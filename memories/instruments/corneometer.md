# Corneometer® — instrument profile

## What it measures
Stratum corneum hydration (electrical capacitance method).

## Unit
Arbitrary units (a.u.), range 0–130.

## Normal ranges
- Dry skin: < 30 a.u.
- Normal skin: 30–50 a.u.
- Well-hydrated: > 50 a.u.

## Practical significance thresholds
- **Minimal perceptible difference:** ~3 a.u. (intra-individual).
- **Standard practical threshold for claims:** ≥ 5 a.u. increase.
- **Strong effect:** ≥ 10 a.u. increase.

## Measurement protocol
- Controlled environment: 22 ± 2°C, 40–60% RH.
- Acclimatisation: 30 min before measurement.
- 3 measurements per site, averaged.
- Preferred site: volar forearm or cheek.

## Historical effect sizes (company benchmarks)
- Standard moisturiser: +5 to +8 a.u. at D28.
- Premium moisturiser: +8 to +12 a.u. at D28.
- Anti-aging cream (hydration secondary): +4 to +7 a.u. at D28.
